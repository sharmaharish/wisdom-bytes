# Research: Content Sources for Wisdom Concepts

## fs.blog (Farnam Street)
- **URL**: https://fs.blog/mental-models/
- **Scope**: ~100 mental models explained
- **Categories**:
  - General Thinking Concepts (10): Probabilistic Thinking, Inversion, Occam's Razor, Hanlon's Razor, etc.
  - Physics, Chemistry & Biology: Thermodynamics, Inertia, Evolution, Ecosystems, etc.
  - Systems: Bottlenecks, Law of Diminishing Returns, etc.
  - Numeracy (14): Regression to Mean, Bayes' Theorem, etc.
  - Microeconomics & Strategy (14): Supply & Demand, Monopoly, etc.
  - Military & War (5): OODA Loop, etc.
  - Human Nature & Judgment (24): Dunning-Kruger, Confirmation Bias, etc.

## lawsofsoftwareengineering.com
- **URL**: https://lawsofsoftwareengineering.com/
- **Scope**: 56 laws organized into 7 parts (book also available)
- **Categories**:
  - Architecture & Complexity (9): Gall's Law, Leaky Abstractions, CAP Theorem, Hyrum's Law
  - People, Teams & Organizations (9): Conway's Law, Brooks' Law, Dunbar's Number, Peter Principle
  - Time, Estimation & Planning (6): Hofstadter's Law, Parkinson's Law, Goodhart's Law
  - Quality, Maintenance & Evolution (11): Murphy's Law, Broken Windows, Technical Debt, Lehman's Laws
  - Scale, Performance & Growth (3): Amdahl's Law, Metcalfe's Law
  - Coding & Design Principles (7): DRY, KISS, YAGNI, SOLID, Demeter
  - Decision-Making & Biases (12): Dunning-Kruger, Hanlon's Razor, Sunk Cost, First Principles

## untools.co
- **URL**: https://untools.co/
- **Scope**: ~25 thinking tools and frameworks
- **Tools**: Six Thinking Hats, Ishikawa Diagram, Eisenhower Matrix, Second-order thinking, Iceberg Model, Abstraction Laddering, Decision Matrix, Impact-Effort Matrix, Connection Circles, Ladder of Inference, OODA Loop, Cynefin Framework, First Principles, Inversion, Issue Trees, and more

## Wikipedia — List of Cognitive Biases
- **URL**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_cognitive_biases
- **Scope**: 200+ cognitive biases
- **Categories**: Association, Baseline, Inertia, Outcome, Self-perspective
- **Examples**: Anchoring, Base Rate Fallacy, Gambler's Fallacy, Hot-hand Fallacy, Conservatism, Outcome Bias, Planning Fallacy, Confirmation Bias, Hindsight Bias, Dunning-Kruger, etc.

## Wikipedia — List of Fallacies
- **URL**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_fallacies
- **Scope**: Extensive list of formal and informal fallacies
- **Categories**: Formal (propositional, quantification, syllogistic), Informal (improper premise, faulty generalizations, questionable cause, relevance)

## Wikipedia — List of Paradoxes
- **URL**: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/List_of_Paradoxes
- **Scope**: Comprehensive list of paradoxes grouped thematically
- **Examples**: Jevons' Paradox, Abilene Paradox, Berkson's Paradox, Paradox of Analysis, etc.

## Threw the Looking Glass — 219 Cognitive Biases
- **URL**: https://threwthelookingglass.com/cognitive-biases/
- **Scope**: 219 cognitive biases, effects, and phenomena
- **Format**: Single-page list with brief descriptions
