Name: Confirmation Bias
Category: cognitive-biases
Tags: reasoning, perception, decision-making

The tendency to search for, interpret, and recall information that confirms one's preexisting beliefs while giving disproportionately less attention to information that contradicts them.

Example: A developer who believes a certain framework is superior will notice every article praising it but scroll past benchmarks showing its competitors perform better.

Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Confirmation_bias
