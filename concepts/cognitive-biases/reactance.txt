Name: Reactance
Category: cognitive-biases
Tags: psychology, autonomy, persuasion, resistance

A psychological response triggered when people perceive their freedom of choice is being threatened or eliminated. The instinctive reaction is to resist and do the opposite of what is being imposed, as a way to reassert autonomy. Reactance explains why heavy-handed persuasion often backfires: the harder you push someone, the more they dig in. Effective influencers preserve the other person's sense of choice rather than issuing commands or ultimatums.

Example: A teenager told they absolutely cannot attend a party becomes obsessed with finding a way to go, even though they had little interest in it before the prohibition. The restriction itself created the desire to rebel.
