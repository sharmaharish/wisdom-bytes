Name: Circle of Concern vs Influence
Category: decision-frameworks
Tags: stoicism, productivity, mindset

From Stephen Covey's "The 7 Habits of Highly Effective People," this framework divides everything into two circles: the Circle of Concern (things you care about but can't control — economy, company layoffs, other people's opinions) and the Circle of Influence (things you can directly affect — your skills, your responses, your work output). Proactive people focus on their Circle of Influence, which eventually expands. Reactive people focus on concerns outside their control, which shrinks their influence.

Example: A developer worried about a potential company restructuring (concern, no influence) can redirect energy to building skills, contributing to open source, and networking (influence). The restructuring anxiety doesn't help; being ready regardless of outcome does. The developer might even influence the outcome by becoming more valuable.
