Name: Gustafson's Law
Category: engineering-laws
Tags: parallel-computing, performance, scaling

It is possible to achieve significant speedup in parallel processing by increasing the problem size. Where Amdahl's Law assumes a fixed problem size and pessimistically concludes speedup is limited, Gustafson's Law observes that when more processors are available, developers tend to increase the problem size to use that extra power.

Example: In high-performance computing, climate modeling on 1,000 CPUs won't just finish 1,000x faster — instead they run a far more detailed global model in the same time, yielding a better forecast. In big data, if analyzing 1 million records takes an hour on one machine, a 10-machine cluster might analyze 10 million records in an hour instead of finishing in 6 minutes.
