Name: Law of the Instrument
Category: engineering-laws
Tags: cognition, bias, tools, decision-making

If the only tool you have is a hammer, you tend to see every problem as a nail. People over-rely on familiar tools and frameworks, applying them to problems where they are suboptimal. This cognitive bias limits creative problem-solving and leads to architectural decisions driven by comfort rather than suitability.

Example: A team that knows microservices well tries to decompose every new application into microservices, even a simple CRUD API that would be better served by a monolith. The familiar tool distorts the solution.
