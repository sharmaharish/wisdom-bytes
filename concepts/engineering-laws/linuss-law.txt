Name: Linus's Law
Category: engineering-laws
Tags: open-source, code-review, quality

Given enough eyeballs, all bugs are shallow. Linus's Law highlights the strength of peer review and community in software development. If a codebase is accessible to many developers, someone will eventually have the expertise to identify and fix a given bug. This is a key advantage of open-source software.

Example: The Apache HTTP Server, used by millions, has a vast user base that helps discover security flaws and bugs rapidly. The infamous Log4Shell vulnerability was identified and patched by the community. In contrast, a proprietary enterprise product with only a few clients might have a subtle bug that takes much longer to surface due to limited "eyeballs."
