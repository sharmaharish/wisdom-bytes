Name: Parkinson's Law
Category: engineering-laws
Tags: time, management, productivity, work

Work expands so as to fill the time available for its completion.

Example: If you give yourself a week to write a 1-hour presentation, it'll take a week. If you give yourself a day, you'll find a way to finish it in a day.

Source: https://lawsofsoftwareengineering.com/laws/parkinsons-law/
