Name: Tiny Habits
Category: heuristics
Tags: behavior-change, productivity, habits, consistency

BJ Fogg's Tiny Habits method argues that lasting behavior change comes from starting so small you can't fail. Instead of committing to an hour of exercise, do one push-up. The core framework is ABC: Anchor (tie the habit to an existing routine), Behavior (make it tiny), Celebration (reinforce with positive emotion). Tiny habits work because they bypass the barriers of motivation and willpower — a habit that takes seconds can be done even when tired or busy. Over time, these micro-behaviors compound into significant change.

Example: To build a writing habit, anchor it to making morning coffee: "After I pour my coffee, I will write one sentence." The tiny behavior removes friction, and over weeks that single sentence naturally grows into paragraphs and pages.
