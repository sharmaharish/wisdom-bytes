Name: Front Lawn vs. Back Lawn
Category: mental-models
Tags: presentation, substance, quality, perception

A metaphor distinguishing between public-facing polish (front lawn) and private, substantive reality (back lawn). The front lawn is the curated image presented to the world—branding, marketing, first impressions—meticulously maintained for show. The back lawn is where real living happens: candid conversations, messy experimentation, genuine relationships. In business, a company with a perfect front lawn (slick website, impressive pitch deck) but a neglected back lawn (toxic culture, broken processes) will ultimately fail. Both matter, but long-term success depends on back lawn health.

Example: A restaurant with a beautiful storefront, stylish menu design, and glowing Instagram feed (front lawn) but mediocre food and chaotic kitchen operations (back lawn) will drive repeat customers away. The kitchen—the back lawn—is what actually determines whether people return.
