Name: Ikigai
Category: mental-models
Tags: purpose, meaning, fulfillment, happiness

A Japanese concept meaning "a reason for being"—the intersection of what you love, what you're good at, what the world needs, and what you can be paid for. Finding your ikigai brings deep fulfillment and is associated with longevity, purpose, and the experience of flow. It is not about grand destiny but about small joys and meaningful daily engagement. The ikigai framework helps individuals and organizations identify where their passions, skills, market needs, and economic viability overlap.

Example: A graphic designer loves illustration (passion), excels at branding (skill), creates logos that help small businesses stand out (world need), and runs a freelance practice that pays the bills (economic). Her work feels energizing rather than draining—that alignment is her ikigai.
