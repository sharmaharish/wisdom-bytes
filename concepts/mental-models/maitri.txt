Name: Maitri
Category: mental-models
Tags: compassion, self-compassion, mindfulness, kindness

A Sanskrit Buddhist concept encompassing benevolence, loving-kindness, and active goodwill toward oneself and others. Pema Chödrön teaches that maitri begins with self-compassion—embracing ourselves as we are, imperfections included—before extending that same warmth outward. It is the antidote to the harsh inner critic and the us-versus-them mentality, replacing them with an open-hearted recognition of shared humanity.

Example: When you make a major mistake at work, instead of spiraling into self-criticism ("I'm incompetent"), you treat yourself with the same kindness you'd offer a friend, acknowledge the error, learn from it, and move forward without shame.
