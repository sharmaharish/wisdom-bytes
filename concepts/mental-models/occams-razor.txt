Name: Occam's Razor
Category: mental-models
Tags: reasoning, simplicity, problem-solving

Entities should not be multiplied without necessity. Among competing hypotheses, the one with the fewest assumptions should be selected.

Example: If you hear hoofbeats outside your window, think horses, not zebras. The simplest explanation — a horse — is more likely than an exotic alternative.
