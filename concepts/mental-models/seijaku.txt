Name: Seijaku
Category: mental-models
Tags: tranquility, focus, creativity, calm

A Japanese concept describing a state of energized calm—serene focus and clarity that persists even amid chaos. Seijaku is not passive relaxation but a dynamic tranquility where one feels centered and capable of decisive action. It is cultivated through mindfulness meditation, purposeful action aligned with values, creating moments of stillness, and balancing work with rest. In practice, it means responding to crises with composure rather than reacting impulsively.

Example: An executive walks into a tense boardroom where quarterly results missed projections and tempers are flaring. Instead of panicking or deflecting blame, she takes a breath, centers herself, and leads the discussion with calm clarity—turning the crisis into a constructive problem-solving session.
