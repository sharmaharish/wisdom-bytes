Name: Sisu
Category: mental-models
Tags: resilience, perseverance, determination, grit

A Finnish concept describing extraordinary determination and courage in the face of adversity. Sisu goes beyond ordinary resilience—it is the "extra mile" attitude of pushing through when you have nothing left, a stoic endurance that refuses to quit. It shaped Finland's national character, famously enabling the outnumbered Finnish army to resist the Soviet invasion during the Winter War. Sisu can be cultivated through goal-setting, mindfulness, physical exercise, embracing failure, and building strong support systems.

Example: A marathon runner hits "the wall" at mile 20, their body screaming to stop. Instead of walking, they find a reservoir of sisu—a quiet, stubborn refusal to give in—and press forward one painful step at a time to the finish line.
