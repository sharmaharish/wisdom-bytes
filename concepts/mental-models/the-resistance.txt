Name: The Resistance
Category: mental-models
Tags: creativity, procrastination, fear, self-sabotage

Steven Pressfield's term for the internal psychological force that sabotages creative work and meaningful pursuit. The Resistance manifests as procrastination, self-doubt, fear of judgment, perfectionism, and distraction—whatever keeps you from sitting down and doing the work that matters most. Pressfield argues the Resistance is strongest when the work is most important to our growth. The cure is not motivation but discipline: showing up daily, treating the work as a professional, and pushing through regardless of how you feel.

Example: A writer sits down each morning to work on their novel. Before opening the document, they find themselves checking email, tidying the desk, scrolling social media—anything but writing. That invisible force steering them away is The Resistance, and the only way past it is to close the distractions and write anyway.
