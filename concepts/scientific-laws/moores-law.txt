Name: Moore's Law
Category: scientific-laws
Tags: computing, transistors, scaling

The number of transistors on a microchip doubles approximately every two years, leading to exponential growth in computational power and a corresponding decrease in cost per transistor. Observed by Gordon Moore in 1965, this empirical trend drove the entire semiconductor industry for decades and enabled the modern computing revolution.

Example: A 2024 smartphone has more computing power than a supercomputer from the 1990s. This exponential scaling made possible innovations like deep learning, real-time video processing, and cloud computing at massive scale.
